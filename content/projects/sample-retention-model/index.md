## Problem

**Sample content.** A team tracking cancelled and reinstated cases needed to look at the same numbers by two different dates, and each broker had to see only their own cases.

## Data model

One wide fact table with two date keys, a tenure dimension, a broker dimension and a small security mapping table. The termination date relationship is inactive and switched on inside the measures.

## Report

Five pages with drillthrough, a tooltip page, and a toggle bar for date basis and count mode. Alt text is generated by measures so it follows the selection.

## Under the hood

Disconnected parameter tables feed `SELECTEDVALUE`; `USERELATIONSHIP` switches the date basis; the broker role maps `USERPRINCIPALNAME()` to the mapping table.

## Outcome

Brokers get a personal view, analysts get a switchable one, and nobody maintains two reports. (Sample text.)
